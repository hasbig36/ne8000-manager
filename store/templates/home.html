Questions

What counts as a device?

Each router you save in the app (Manager › Routers). Switches and resellers drawn on maps and plans are free.

Which routers does it support?

Huawei NetEngine 8000 M8, M14 and F series, NE40E and other VRP8 routers.

Does the hosted server reach my routers?

The server needs to reach your routers' management IPs over SSH and SNMP. Allow its public IP on your management ACL, or connect it over a VPN. We help you set this up.

What happens if I do not renew?

You get a renewal invoice 7 days before the end of the period and a 7-day grace period after it. After that the app stops connecting to routers until you renew. Your data is kept.

Can I pay in shillings?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars and M-Pesa payments are charged in KES at about {{ '%g'|format(kes_rate) }} KES per dollar.
